##Rong Tuli AI##

An AI-powered business team for micro-entrepreneurs.

«AI shouldn't replace entrepreneurs. It should give them leverage.»

Rong Tuli AI is an AI-native business platform designed to give micro-businesses access to capabilities normally available only to larger companies — including customer support, sales assistance, product discovery, inventory intelligence, marketing support, and business insights.

The project was born from Rong Tuli, a real handmade clothing business operated by a student founder. Instead of building a fictional business case, we are using a real small business as the first environment for testing whether AI can meaningfully increase the operating capacity of an entrepreneur.

##

The Problem

A large company can afford specialized teams for:

- Sales
- Customer support
- Marketing
- Operations
- Inventory
- Business analytics

A micro-entrepreneur often has to do all of these jobs alone.

They may have the skill and the product, but not the infrastructure needed to scale.

Rong Tuli AI explores a different model:

«Give one entrepreneur an AI-powered team.»

##

What Rong Tuli AI Does

The platform is designed around specialized AI agents that assist with different parts of the business.

🤖 AI Sales Agent

Understands natural-language customer requests and helps:

- Identify customer requirements
- Search relevant products
- Check availability
- Recommend products
- Assist with sales workflows

💬 Customer Support Agent

Helps entrepreneurs handle repetitive customer interactions while escalating situations that require human judgment.

📊 Business Intelligence Agent

Analyzes business information to identify:

- Product performance
- Customer demand
- Inventory patterns
- Potential opportunities
- Actionable business insights

📣 Marketing Agent

Assists with:

- Product descriptions
- Promotional content
- Customer follow-ups
- Campaign ideas
- Product-specific messaging

The LLM is not given unrestricted access to business data.

Gemini provides reasoning and determines what information or action is needed, while the application backend controls available tools and business operations.

This creates a controlled boundary between AI reasoning and real-world business actions.

##

Why We Built It

Rong Tuli AI started from a real business rather than a hypothetical problem.

Rong Tuli is a small handmade clothing business that sells primarily through offline customers.

Running it revealed how much operational work exists behind even a small business.

As a student with limited financial resources and no conventional development computer, I also experienced the difficulty of building without the infrastructure that larger teams take for granted.

That led to a simple question:

«What if the smallest businesses could access the capabilities of a much larger business through AI?»

Rong Tuli AI is our attempt to explore that question.

##

Built With Limited Resources

This project was developed primarily from a tablet, without a conventional local development setup.

That created challenges across:

- Coding
- Debugging
- Testing
- Cloud development
- Project management
- Deployment

Instead of treating those limitations as a reason not to build, we used cloud-based development and AI-assisted tools to keep moving.

The constraint reinforced our product philosophy:

«Technology should create leverage for people who have limited resources.»

- Handmade sellers
